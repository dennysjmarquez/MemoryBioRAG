usage: biorag listar [--pagina N]

Summary:
List permanent concepts in pages of 10 with content previews.

What is it for?
Browse permanent concepts page by page when you do not yet know what to search for.

  - `corteza` (cortex: permanent memory)
  - `sueno` (sleep: consolidation)
  - `sustantivos clave` (key nouns: the core topic of a memory)

Options:
  --pagina N              Page number, starting at 1.

Examples:
  biorag listar
  biorag listar --pagina 2

Expected output:
A page of permanent concept names and content previews.
Runtime messages retain their existing language (often Spanish); JSON keys are unchanged.

See also:
  biorag buscar --help
  biorag guardar --help
  biorag sueno --help
